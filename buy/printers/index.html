    Printer comparison¶

    Specs were copied from manufacturer pages in late 2026. The date on each row is last_checked. A dash is not zero. It means that page did not state the value, or two sentences on the same page disagreed.

    Best for and skill are our judgment from those specs. They are not a review score.

    The machine-readable copy, including every note, is data/printers.json. How to read a row before you spend money is What to buy.

    These four short lists are judgments from the specs below, not a lab test and not the manufacturer's ranking.

    • Best for beginners

      Bambu Lab A1 mini, Bambu Lab A1, Flashforge AD5X. Open machines with a low from-price, or a multicolor printer that already includes the color unit.

    • Best for multicolor

      Flashforge AD5X, Creality Hi, Snapmaker U1, Bambu Lab H2C. A built-in color system, a filament changer, or several toolheads.

    • Best for engineering filament

      Bambu Lab H2S, Bambu Lab X2D, Creality K2 Plus, Prusa CORE One+. A published heated chamber, or a high nozzle temperature on an enclosed machine.

    • Best for big prints

      Creality K2 Plus, Bambu Lab H2S, Bambu Lab H3C. The largest build volumes in this table.

    Dashes mean the source page did not state that value. Prices are the from-price or sale price in the note, in USD, and they move.

    Price notes and conflicts

    Bambu Lab A1 mini. From $199 USD on the US store. Typical price shown as $219. Combo prices are higher. Spec page: PLA, PETG, TPU, and PVA ideal; ABS, ASA, PC, PA, and fiber-filled filaments not recommended. US store calls the A series open-frame, which is why enclosed and heated_chamber are false. Max speed of toolhead.

    Bambu Lab A1. From $259 USD on the US store. Typical price shown as $299. Spec page: PLA, PETG, TPU, and PVA ideal; ABS, ASA, PC, PA, and fiber-filled filaments not recommended. US store calls the A series open-frame. Max speed of toolhead.

    Bambu Lab P1S. Current standalone US price was not verified on 2026-11-07; check the manufacturer or an authorized retailer. Factory-reference specifications, not the owner's installed nozzle or current operating status. Manufacturer lists PLA, PETG, TPU, ABS, ASA, PVA and PET as ideal, PA and PC as capable, and carbon/glass-fiber polymers as not recommended with the stock stainless-steel nozzle. Active chamber heating is not specified in the cited technical sheet, so heated_chamber and chamber_max_c are null. Maximum toolhead speed, not a guaranteed printing speed.

    Bambu Lab P2S. From $449 USD on the US store. Typical price shown as $549. Spec page: outer frame plastic and glass, activated-carbon filter, door sensor. It does not list an active chamber-heater temperature, so heated_chamber is null. US store calls the P2S enclosed. Max speed of toolhead.

    Bambu Lab X1 Carbon. Not listed on the Bambu Lab US store collection page fetched 2026-11-07. Hardened steel nozzle and extruder gears; Micro-LiDAR and a 1080p monitoring camera. Bed temperature depends on supply voltage. Maximum toolhead travel speed: 500 mm/s; actual print speed depends on the material and profile.

    Bambu Lab X2D. From $589 USD on the US store, 2026-11-07. Typical price shown as $649. The April 2026 launch post listed a printer-only US price of $649 and a combo at $899. Nozzle, bed, speed, and build volumes are from the X2D spec page. Active chamber heating to 65 °C is from Bambu's 14 April 2026 launch post. Current price is from the US store, not the launch post. Max speed of toolhead on the spec page.

    Bambu Lab H2S. From $1,049 USD on the US store. Typical price shown as $1,199. Laser editions cost more. Active chamber heating to 65 °C is on the spec page. A laser edition exists; this row is the printer, not the laser tool. Max speed of toolhead.

    Bambu Lab H2D. From $1,399 USD on the US store. Typical price shown as $1,549. Active chamber heating to 65 °C is on the spec page. Laser and cutting modules are optional editions, not part of this row's price. Max speed of toolhead.

    Bambu Lab H2C. From $1,899 USD on the US store. Typical price shown as $2,199. Vortek changes hotends on the right tool position; the left hotend remains fixed. Six interchangeable right-side hotends plus the fixed left hotend support seven assigned materials before additional AMS configurations. Max speed of toolhead on the spec page.

    Bambu Lab H3C. From $2,499 USD on the US store. No separate typical price was shown on the collection page. Enclosure type Enclosed and active chamber heating to 65 °C are on the spec page. Max speed of toolhead.

    Creality Hi. Manufacturer US listing: $299 printer only; $399 Combo with CFS. Both listed as sold out; verify availability. Nozzle ≤ 300 °C and heatbed ≤ 100 °C. The page does not say whether the frame is enclosed, so enclosed and heated_chamber are null. Supported printer filaments listed: Hyper-PLA, PLA, PETG, ABS, PLA-CF. Max speed 500 mm/s. Creality also states a typical speed of 300 mm/s.

    Creality K1C. Creality's K1 compare page shows both $529 and $559. The checkout price was not explicit in the extract, so price_usd is null. Build volume, CoreXY, and the two prices are from the compare page. Nozzle 300 °C, heatbed 100 °C, and 600 mm/s are also on the K1C support page. Enclosure and a multi-material system were not stated on those pages. Compare page: max 600 mm/s. It also lists a typical speed of 300 mm/s.

    Creality K2 Plus. Creality store collection shows $999 and $1,299 for the K2 Plus. The Combo is listed separately at $1,199 and $1,499. price_usd is the lower printer-only figure. Volume, speeds, nozzle 350 °C, bed 120 °C, and chamber 60 °C are from Creality's K2 Plus FAQ. Active chamber heating is why enclosed is true. The buying guide's frame row was internally inconsistent in the extract, so motion is null. FAQ: 600 mm/s max speed and 30,000 mm/s² max acceleration.

    Snapmaker U1. The spec page fetched 2026-11-07 did not show a USD price. ABS, ASA, PA, and PC are listed only with the optional top cover. Carbon-fiber filament also needs that cover and a hardened steel nozzle. No active chamber heater is stated, so heated_chamber is false and enclosed is false for the base machine. Max toolhead speed.

    Snapmaker J1s. Manufacturer comparison lists $1,399 MSRP. This is a reference MSRP, not a live checkout quote. Page says independent dual extruders. Supported materials include PLA, ABS, PC, TPU above 90A, PETG, ASA, PA, PA-GF, and PA-CF. Hardened nozzle for PA and fiber fills. Enclosure and top speed were not stated. Manufacturer maximum printing speed: 350 mm/s; quality and material profiles may use lower speeds.

    Snapmaker Artisan. Manufacturer comparison lists $2,999 MSRP for the 3-in-1 configuration. Module bundles and current checkout pricing may differ. The fetched spec page lists a dual-nozzle work area and separate machine-and-enclosure dimensions, but it did not state nozzle temperature, bed temperature, chamber heat, or a print-speed limit. Those fields are null. Manufacturer comparison lists 300 mm/s maximum printing speed; the detailed Artisan specification lists 180 mm/s as its recommended printing speed.

    Flashforge AD5X. Sale price $349 USD. Regular price shown as $499. CoreXY is stated. Recommended filaments include PETG, PLA, TPU 95A for single color, TPU 64D–75D, PLA-CF, and PETG-CF. The page does not say the machine is enclosed. Page states max speed 600 mm/s and max print speed 300 mm/s. Travel speed is also listed as max 600 mm/s.

    Flashforge Creator 5 Pro. The product page fetched 2026-11-07 did not show a USD price. HEPA13 plus activated carbon. TPU 90A is listed for the top external mount. Recommended filaments include ABS, ASA, PA-CF, and PPS-CF. Max travel speed 600 mm/s. Max print speed stated as 300 mm/s.

    Prusa CORE One+. Assembled from $1,299 on the Prusa comparison table. Kit price was not taken from that same row. The CORE One+ vs MK4S table on Prusa's site lists CoreXY, enclosed chamber, active heating up to 55 °C, and nozzle 290 °C or 400 °C with the HT hotend. Bed 120 °C is the CORE One column on the MK4S product comparison. This row is the stock 290 °C hotend. A single maximum linear print speed is not listed in the cited product specification. Use the PrusaSlicer material and quality preset; model print times are more useful for comparison.

    Prusa MK4S. Assembled from $999 on Prusa's CORE One+ comparison. A kit price of $729 appeared on a separate MMU3-bundle page and is not the price_usd. MK4S product page: build volume 250 × 210 × 220 mm, nozzle 290 °C, heatbed 120 °C, Cartesian is the kinematics label on Prusa's comparison. ABS and ASA are listed when using the optional enclosure. A single maximum linear print speed is not listed in the cited product specification. Use the PrusaSlicer material and quality preset; model print times are more useful for comparison.

    Elegoo Centauri Carbon. The product page showed $438.41 USD at fetch time. That is not a round list price, so confirm it at checkout. The manufacturer’s user manual specifies a 110°C maximum heated bed. Enclosed chamber with a 320°C hardened nozzle, automatic calibration and a monitoring camera. Up to 500 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s².

    Anycubic Kobra S1. Sale price $349 USD. Regular price shown as $549. Page says fully enclosed CoreXY. Supporting filaments listed: PLA, PETG, TPU, ABS, ASA. Active drying is listed as not supported. No chamber-heater temperature is stated. Maximum 600 mm/s. Recommended 300 mm/s.

    QIDI Plus4. Sale price $649 USD. Regular price shown as $799. Dedicated manufacturer technical specifications used; older product-page body text contains conflicting speed and bed-temperature figures. Manufacturer maximum toolhead speed: 600 mm/s. Actual printing speed is limited by the material and slicing profile.

    Creality K3. Check the manufacturer for current regional pricing. Manufacturer maximum print speed; actual speed depends on material and geometry.

    Creality SPARKX i8. Check the manufacturer for current regional pricing. Manufacturer maximum print speed; actual speed depends on material and geometry.
