    Fleet comparison¶

    Six printers: Bambu X1C, Bambu X2D, Creality Hi, Creality K2 Plus, Snapmaker U1, Flashforge Creator 5 Pro. Inventory notes: one K2 Plus with two CFS units; the X1C has a Panda Breath chamber heater, two "AMS Pro" units and one AMS.

    Read these caveats before trusting a number

    • Maximum temperatures are limits, not recommended settings. Use the material matrix and the filament label.
    • Suggested uses are practical judgments based on the hardware, not benchmark rankings.
    • Tool changers avoid repeatedly unloading one nozzle, but they can still need priming or a prime tower. AMS/CFS switching usually needs purge material.
    • Material compatibility depends on the specific filament, nozzle, drying, enclosure, and profile.
    • No automatic transfer of the retired P1S's AMS to the Hi is assumed.

    To verify before publishing: "AMS Pro" naming

    "AMS Pro" is my own label for those units. Bambu Lab's current wiki names its units AMS, AMS 2 Pro, AMS HT, and AMS lite (see Bambu Lab Wiki: Filament Drying Recommendations, checked 2026-10-31). Check the model label on each unit and change "AMS Pro" to the official name (probably AMS 2 Pro) before sharing. The same page says AMS 2 Pro units on an X1 need their own power adapter for drying, while the X2D can power one from the printer.
