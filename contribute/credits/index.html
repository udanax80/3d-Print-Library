    Third-party guides¶

    AD3D (@ad3duae) guides: permission needed before publishing

    Several pages (Slicer tips, Material notes) are organized from instructional visuals posted by AD3D (@ad3duae). This public site contains my own written summaries only. The original images are not republished here.

    Before making this site public: ask AD3D for permission to share the summaries (and to link or embed the visuals, if wanted), or rewrite those sections from primary sources (Bambu Lab Wiki, Prusa KB). Keep the credit line either way.

    Other references are linked, not copied: Bambu Lab Wiki, Prusa Knowledge Base and blog, OrcaSlicer Wiki, Ellis' Print Tuning Guide, UL Chemical Insights, and manufacturer spec pages. Each page cites what it used.
